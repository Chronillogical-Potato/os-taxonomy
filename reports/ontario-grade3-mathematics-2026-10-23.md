# Ontario Grade 3 Mathematics — 23 October 2026

The Grade 3 pass maps the official 2020 curriculum onto the shared taxonomy after a reuse audit. All 58 retained expectations were checked against staging: 46 specific and 12 overall. Source wording, source IDs and stored hierarchy are unchanged. [Official Ontario source](https://www.dcp.edu.gov.on.ca/en/curriculum/elementary-mathematics/grades/g3-math).

**No topics were added.** The pass adds 155 Grade 3 topic-code pairs across 114 existing topics. Forty-eight codes are directly linked; ten overall expectations roll up their children. The resulting graph has **1,838 topics / 3,887 dependencies**. All old topic-code pairs, including 152 Grade 1 and 124 Grade 2 Mathematics pairs, remain intact.

Review removed stale “age 9+” suffixes from the fraction-as-sums and unit-relationship titles. Country-specific ages remain in the source alignments; assessed descriptions and evidence are unchanged by these title edits. “Unit relationships and conversion tables” remains distinct from the existing “Converting measurement units” procedure.

The existing congruence concept (`mt_B1lPmwJz0h`) now covers matching in two and three dimensions. Its former two-dimensional side/angle, overlay, rotation and size criteria are retained. New evidence covers matching faces, edges and corner angles, and deciding whole-object congruence. Two boxes with the same front face but different depths are a deliberate non-example. Volume formulas and formal congruence criteria are outside this claim. This topic had no learner estimates, evidence or teaching-research record before extension; no learner resets were needed.

Ten old hard prerequisites become supportive soft links where they imposed a particular method, unrelated unit family or specialist vocabulary. Seven hard and two soft prerequisites are added for actual foundations. The repairs concern the mean, clock reading, perimeter, observable-feature grouping, classification keys and unit conversion. Topic bodies elsewhere remain unchanged. Existing learner records, assessment history and teaching research are preserved.

Two additional source-backed component links support the foundations: Ontario kindergarten `ON-K-2026-K-B13.2` on observable-feature grouping (`mt_AylKwhbDWM`), and Common Core `3.OA.2` on division as equal sharing/grouping (`mt_LpSuPgL31x`). They represent genuine existing source clauses. No other non-Ontario pair changes.

The final Canadian hard-prerequisite closure contains 172 topics and 204 edges, with no missing or later-age prerequisite in that closure. All seven new hard edges pass country/age checks; no new hard-edge country/age conflicts are introduced elsewhere. Historical baseline issues outside this closure are not recertified. Independent checks confirm graph/DB agreement, derived age bounds and centrality, refreshed congruence embedding and an acyclic graph.

Coverage is a semantic authoring judgment: all 46 specific and 12 overall expectations are judged Full through the listed claims or rollups. A link or success on one task does not certify a learner's mastery of every linked topic. For example, the three-attribute sorting route uses an existing classification-tree claim in a living-things context; a mathematics sorting task does not certify the full biology claim. Unit-conversion examples do not make calendar facts a universal prerequisite. This pass does not recertify other Ontario documents or historical Australian coverage judgments.

No teaching research was added. The public research projection retains 1,049 records and explicitly lists 789 topics without research; congruence remains among those gaps. The Grade 2 report remains a historical record of that authoring pass; its newly added congruence topic is the same stable ID extended here.
