Applied through the authoritative taxonomy builder and staging database on 23 October 2026. This local export matches the independently verified staging checkpoint. Earlier Australian authoring reports describe historical snapshots.

## Reason for the cleanup

The retrospective reviewed all 314 Australian additions and 14 later edited IDs (five overlap the additions, giving 323 distinct records). It identified repeated skills authored for different grades, media, or contexts, plus bundled mastery requirements and some genuine additions.

The initial review classified 160 additions as distinct, 45 as merge candidates, 39 as extensions before possible retirement, six as localization before possible retirement, 47 as splits/redistributions, and 17 as uncertain. Those are original review categories, not final deletion counts. Subsequent discussion and delegated decisions gave all 17 uncertain cases a treatment direction.

Current mapping counts and remaining limitations are recorded below and in the linked verification records.

## Mastery and learner records

Each retained topic has one mastery claim; this cleanup does not introduce separately tracked difficulty levels. Distinct number-system demands, procedures, and bodies of knowledge can remain separate. Grade, country, or software medium alone does not establish a new skill.

Learner evidence is preserved separately from duplicate age-derived priors. Priors are not added or averaged during consolidation. The user explicitly approved neutralizing 52 inherited estimates on two expanded number topics pending reassessment; the actual learner observation and all assessment history remain intact. Private learner backups are not included in this repository.
