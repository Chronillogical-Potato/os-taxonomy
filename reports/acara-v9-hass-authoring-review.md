# Shared taxonomy and Australian HASS coverage

> Historical authoring snapshot. Its counts and Full labels describe the original contribution, not the current release. See the [23 October cleanup](acara-v9-cleanup-2026-10-23.md) and [current mapping summary](acara-v9-scope-coverage.md).

Reviewed 21 October 2026. The Australian HASS curriculum maps onto shared
subjects; HASS is not a taxonomy subject. All 89 Foundation–Year 6 standards are
Full in the [coverage report](acara-v9-hass-coverage.md), using 210 links across
124 topics: 70 existing topics and 54 additions.

The [cross-country authoring policy](../README.md#adding-curricula) calls for shared
subjects and domains, reuse or extension of matching topics, and individual
teachable concepts or skills. Country-specific knowledge belongs in topics where
it genuinely differs.

## Subject and domain placement

| Subject | Domains used by this batch |
|---|---|
| History | Historical Thinking; Family & Community History; Modern History |
| Geography | Places & Environments; Maps & Spatial Skills |
| Personal & Social Development | Responsible Decision-Making; Empathy & Social Awareness |
| Life Skills | Entrepreneurship |

Geography is a reusable discipline. The new History domains support learning
across countries; Australian colonial history, Federation and citizenship changes
retain their specific factual content within Modern History. Cultural diversity
uses the existing Personal & Social Development framework.

## Reuse and focused topics

All 51 initial draft topics were compared with the existing graph using text
similarity, keyword searches and content review. Two overlaps were consolidated
into existing topics, keeping their published IDs:

| Existing topic | ID | Extension |
|---|---|---|
| Evidence from the Past | `mt_9REmUc8r4D` | Interpret and compare old and modern objects using observable evidence. |
| Different perspectives and accounts | `mt_2GDBmKCJxs` | Compare perspectives on objects, places and events. |

Their research-citation evidence was replaced with observable learner tasks.
Related but distinct learning stays separate, with prerequisite links where useful.

Bundled requirements were split into focused topics: picture timelines and
picture maps; Australian administrative divisions, First Nations Countries and
locating neighbouring countries; national commemorations, symbols and religious
or cultural festivals. Timelines use History/Historical Thinking; map skills use
Geography/Maps & Spatial Skills. The resulting batch adds 54 topics.

Three existing map topics move from Mathematics/Geometry to Geography/Maps &
Spatial Skills, with all curriculum links retained:

- Finding places on a plan (`mt_61Pa4Eksva`).
- Reading and drawing maps (`mt_w4cUnN_SsQ`).
- Grid references and directions (`mt_ejht12jhm2`).

## Verification and scope

The authoring graph, persisted mirror, database and export agree. All existing
topic IDs, curriculum links and prerequisite edges are preserved. The batch adds
131 prerequisite edges; it introduces no cycles, age-span warnings or new hard
prerequisite age conflicts. Derived centrality is refreshed. Teaching-research
records are unchanged; coverage identifies the topics without research.

HASS completes this learning area in the current Australian Foundation–Year 6
scope. Health, Digital Technologies and Design and Technologies still have gaps.
PE, Arts and Languages are excluded from the coverage goal.
