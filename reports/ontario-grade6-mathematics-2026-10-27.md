# Ontario Grade 6 Mathematics — 27 October 2026

The Grade 6 pass follows a reuse audit of all **63 retained expectations: 51 specific and 12 overall**. Source IDs, wording, hashes, types and stored hierarchy are unchanged. [Official Ontario source](https://www.dcp.edu.gov.on.ca/en/curriculum/elementary-mathematics/grades/g6-math).

The release adds **244 topic-code pairs on 178 topics: 173 existing and five new**. Fifty-three codes are directly linked; ten overall expectations roll up their stored children. The verified graph and staging DB contain **1,850 topics / 3,967 dependencies / 5,203 known pairs**, plus the same 41 legacy graph pairs. Every earlier pair remains, including Grade 1–5 Mathematics counts of 152 / 124 / 156 / 186 / 190.

## Five additions, with different meanings

- **Divisibility rules** fills an explicit procedure gap for the rule family (2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 9, 10). Place value and known multiples support explanations. There is no separate topic per divisor and no million-range reading gate.
- **Building objects from views** fills the inverse spatial-construction procedure: satisfy and check given top/front/side constraints, including ambiguous views. It does not broaden the Grade 5 drawing/matching claim or promise a unique reconstruction or universal height algorithm.
- **Diagonals of quadrilaterals** extracts focused assessment from a broader existing geometry claim. This is existing mathematical knowledge, not an absent mathematical idea. Learners investigate equality, bisection and perpendicularity; inclusive shape classes and non-rhombus kite counterexamples prevent false universal statements. The older geometry claim is unchanged.
- **Comparing ways to pay** adds financial decision content beyond recognising payment methods. Given scenarios support different criteria and methods; credit knowledge is optional when another legitimate comparison route is used.
- **Interest rates and bank fees** adds financial content about rates, periods and charges on fictional account/loan information. Fees need not be flat; a monthly fee per month can stay fixed while total fees increase over more months. Current rates, compulsory compound-interest calculations and actual product recommendations are outside the claim.

No old topic description, evidence, question, title, type, subject, domain or other non-alignment attribute changed. No range, grade, difficulty or country clones, merges, retirements or learner resets were applied.

## Reuse and the source compositions

Existing histograms include intervals and boundary conventions; connected-line graphs already show time trends. Existing mean/median/mode/range, surface-area and polygon-decomposition claims are reused. The donating proposal was withdrawn: the official examples allow donated time, already represented by community contribution, combined with trade and lender/borrower perspectives. A barter contributor remains explicitly unlinked because a later first Canadian context would create strict age flags; no dummy earlier age is invented.

Several rows use worked combinations rather than asserting every source facet inside a single holder. Division by a decimal uses common units: 234 L ÷ 0.6 L = 2,340 tenths ÷ 6 tenths = 390 groups. In 235 L, 391 whole groups leave 0.4 L, or two-thirds of a group. Dividing by a proper fraction similarly uses thirds as common units. Factor trees record repeated factor-pair splits stopped at primes. Positive-coefficient like terms use distinct labelled tiles and distributivity (2x + 3x + 4y = 5x + 4y); unlike letters remain separate. Joint independent-event outcomes use systematic listing, favourable/total and experiments rather than requiring fraction multiplication along a tree.

Rounding consumes a given recurring expansion; generating it by long division is background. Surface-area transfer beyond the existing rectangular/triangular-base scope is explicit through face identification, polygon decomposition and summing areas. The older net claim is not silently reinterpreted as covering every base. Clockwise/counterclockwise vocabulary is attributed to the existing direction claim, combined with protractor measurement and full-turn relations. Efficient-code contexts reuse loops and execution counts. Seventeen composition rows retain worked tasks and limits; a component task does not certify an entire broader topic.

All 63 rows have a Full contextual authoring judgment. This is separate from learner attainment and school reporting. Ontario retains SEL learning support while excluding the SEL overall expectation from assessment, evaluation and reporting; A1 associations here preserve support/process content. [Official strand note](https://www.dcp.edu.gov.on.ca/en/curriculum/elementary-mathematics/grades/g6-math/strands).
