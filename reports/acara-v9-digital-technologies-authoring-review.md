## Shared framework and reuse

Computing gains six generic domains alongside Artificial Intelligence:

- Digital Systems
- Data Representation
- Algorithms & Programming
- Designing Digital Solutions
- Digital Tools & Collaboration
- Privacy & Security

These domains apply across countries. No subject is added. Three existing topics
move from Artificial Intelligence to a matching domain, retaining their IDs,
content, alignments and prerequisite links:

| Topic | ID | Domain |

| Computers in Everyday Life | `mt_XbGfVhfiUz` | Digital Systems |

Existing Mathematics topics teach number algorithms with decisions and repetition;
the new Computing topics teach implementation, input, variables and testing in
visual programs. Existing English topics supply digital creation, navigation,
research and presentations. General design, citizenship and safety topics are
reused; new topics add digital-specific design, collaboration and privacy skills.
The 74 mapped topics include 36 existing and 38 new topics.

Required practical skills use observable tasks: operating devices and peripherals,
creating and testing programs, comparing designs, changing account settings,
logging in, co-editing documents and planning digital projects. The six previously
Full rows were rechecked against their linked topic content.

## Prerequisite review

Understanding internal components does not require peripheral-device skills.
Writing a first sequence program does not require prior branching and repetition.
Those relationships are soft support; the hard prerequisites use basic components
and sequential instructions instead. Later topics introduce loops and decisions.
