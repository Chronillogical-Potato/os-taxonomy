# Australian Curriculum v9: selected Health coverage

Reviewed 21 October 2026 against the 32 Foundation–Year 6 content descriptions in the Personal, social and community health strand. The mapping adds 84 alignments across 52 existing topics.

This report covers selected Health content. The 27 Movement and physical activity (PE) standards are excluded; the results do not represent coverage of the full Health and Physical Education curriculum.

**11 standards are fully covered, 19 partly covered, and 2 missing.** Full means the linked topics collectively cover every required part at the expected level, including practice or rehearsal where required. Individual links may cover only part of a standard.

| Year band | Full | Partial | Missing | Total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Foundation | 2 | 3 | 1 | 6 |
| Years 1–2 | 2 | 3 | 1 | 6 |
| Years 3–4 | 3 | 7 | 0 | 10 |
| Years 5–6 | 4 | 6 | 0 | 10 |
| **Total** | **11** | **19** | **2** | **32** |
