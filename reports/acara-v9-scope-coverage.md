## Scope and claim

This covers Australian Curriculum v9 Foundation–Year 6 content descriptions for Mathematics, English, Science, HASS, Digital Technologies, Design and Technologies, and the Personal, social and community health strand of Health and Physical Education.

The 27 Movement and physical activity (PE) standards are excluded. Arts, Languages, elaborations and achievement standards are also outside this review. This supports a coverage claim for the listed learning areas, rather than the entire Australian Curriculum.

Curriculum areas map onto shared taxonomy subjects and domains. HASS, Health and Design and Technologies do not create separate subject trees. See the [cross-country authoring policy](../README.md#adding-curricula).
