# Ontario Grade 5 Mathematics — 24 October 2026

The Grade 5 pass maps the official 2020 curriculum after a reuse audit of all 63 retained expectations: 51 specific and 12 overall. Source IDs, wording, hashes and stored hierarchy are unchanged. [Official Ontario source](https://www.dcp.edu.gov.on.ca/en/curriculum/elementary-mathematics/grades/g5-math).

The pass adds **190 Grade 5 topic-code pairs on 148 topics: 145 existing and three new**. Fifty-three codes are directly linked; ten overall expectations roll up their stored children. The resulting graph has **1,845 topics / 3,941 dependencies**. Every old pair remains; Grade 1–4 Mathematics retain 152 / 124 / 156 / 186 pairs, and the same 41 unresolved legacy graph pairs are preserved.

Only one addition represents a missing mathematical representation: **stacked-bar graphs**. It teaches cumulative segments, reading a segment's amount as the difference between endpoints, totals, keys and scales. Count and relative-frequency forms are one representation. It remains distinct from the grouped side-by-side bars added in Grade 4.

**Types of taxes and who collects them** fills a financial/civic content gap rather than a new mathematical operation. It distinguishes income, purchases and property as tax bases, uses collecting governments appropriate to the learner's context, allows overlapping collecting levels where applicable, and connects pooled revenue to public facilities, services and programs. It does not replace the existing general tax/public-services claim, generalize Australian institutional facts to Canada, or prescribe current rates or financial decisions.

**Top, front and side views of objects** extracts focused mathematical assessment from an existing Design & Technologies drawing bundle. It uses an agreed front orientation, provided objects or cube models, drawing and matching. Making 3-D models, CAD and exploded views are supportive contexts, not mandatory prerequisites. The older technical-design claim remains whole with a supportive link to the extraction.

Two initial proposals were withdrawn after inspecting actual scopes. Generic classification covers triangles even when its examples use quadrilaterals; side/angle properties and construction compose the source. Non-standard angle measurement composes existing unit-angle iteration, matching and comparison. The mode already handles several tied modes and no-mode cases, and existing long multiplication connects area models to algorithms. Division's area-model connection is represented through a worked composition: 372 = 12×30 + 12×1, relating partial areas to the long-division quotient 31. No numerical-range, country, difficulty or classroom-project copies were added.

Nine old hard gates become supportive soft links. Four hard and four soft dependencies are added on existing topics; the three new claims add six hard, seven soft and one incoming soft link. Repairs remove unnecessary general fraction-multiplication, sampling-question, simplification, quadrilateral-hierarchy and rotation gates, and use a minimal existing fraction meaning or probability-scale foundation where appropriate. Regularity retains basic shape/property knowledge without requiring informal length measurement; translations and reflections retain their actual existing foundations without first requiring rotation. All existing topic descriptions, evidence, questions, types, subjects, domains and titles are unchanged.

Review added two necessary hard foundations to the full stacked-bar claim: fractions as parts of a whole and percentages as parts per hundred. Count and relative-frequency forms remain one representation; experimental probability is not a prerequisite.

The actual Canadian closure contains **148 holders / 268 topics / 338 hard edges**, with zero missing or later-age prerequisite issues. All ten new hard edges pass every source country. Neither the readiness rule (minimum applicable country/UNIVERSAL start) nor the strict country validator introduces a newly affected edge/country context. The full hard and soft graph is acyclic. The pre-existing US rounding/place-value issue remains unchanged. Derived age bounds, centrality, embedding presence, every graph/DB field and all 4,959 known pairs are independently verified.

All 51 specific and 12 overall rows are judged Full through whole claims, contextual components or child rollups. These are authoring judgments, not learner certifications. Several rows use combinations rather than a single topic asserting every clause: multiplying by 0.1, division models, relative-frequency tables, sales-tax transactions, coordinate scales, visual algebra, transfer roles, triangle classification and custom angle units. A component task does not certify every facet of a broader linked topic. This pass does not recertify other Ontario documents or earlier Australian coverage judgments.

No learner, assessment or teaching-research records were changed. The public research projection retains **1,049 exact records** and the unchanged historical archive. The three new claims increase explicit missing-research coverage from 793 to **796 topics**. The public source catalogue remains 17 documents / 7,743 expectations, including nine Ontario documents / 3,862 expectations published as codes and official links only.
